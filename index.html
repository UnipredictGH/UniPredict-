About

About UniPredict Ghana

Our Mission

UniPredict Ghana is Ghana's most comprehensive university admission intelligence platform. We help WASSCE students make informed decisions about their university applications by providing accurate eligibility checks, verified cut-off points, and personalised programme recommendations.

We cover all accredited Ghanaian universities with over 1,000 programmes, using the official WASSCE aggregate formula to give you precise, programme-specific eligibility results.
